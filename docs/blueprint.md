# **App Name**: Dravida Heritage

## Core Features:

- Heritage Concierge: An AI-powered concierge tool that suggests dishes based on dietary preferences and narrates the historical significance of South Indian ingredients like Fermented Rice and traditional spices.
- Vintage Visual Menu: An immersive digital menu showcasing signature idlis, dosas, and vadas with high-resolution imagery and elegant scroll-based transitions.
- Fluid Reservations: A streamlined booking interface for securing tables, featuring real-time availability and calendar integration.
- Culinary Odyssey Gallery: An interactive section that tells the story of South Indian cuisine through high-quality vintage photography and smooth-reveal animations.
- Live Kitchen Pulse: A status component providing live updates on kitchen occupancy and expected wait times for popular breakfast items.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Muted Bronze (#CCA966) evoking the feel of traditional South Indian brassware and vintage elegance.
- Background color: Deep Sepia Carbon (#1F1D1A), a dark, desaturated brown that provides a high-end, premium gallery atmosphere.
- Accent color: Deep Vermillion (#B83E2E), an analogous warm tone used sparingly for call-to-actions to represent traditional spice aesthetics.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', a high-contrast serif that provides a classic, fashion-forward vintage feel; paired with 'PT Sans', a humanist sans-serif for readable, warm body text.
- Thin-line, minimalist icons with gold-leaf finishes representing traditional motifs like banana leaves, grinding stones, and steam.
- A wide, editorial-inspired layout with significant whitespace and overlapping imagery to mimic the pages of a high-end food magazine.
- Sophisticated, slow-motion 'curtain' transitions between pages and inertial scrolling effects for the visual menu to ensure a premium feel.
